# World spawn

A new world spawn installs a **required pack of vens**.

Some of those vens have a **set spawn point**.

Most spawn **somewhere with these rules** — not a pinned coordinate. Example energy: world A has 24 of ven-456, place them near vens with `prop:canDwell`.

That “somewhere” is checkout + dreaming together: the dwell slot has to be free, and the ven does not need a fully written life until a narrative push or a player gets close.
