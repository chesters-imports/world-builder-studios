# Dreaming

If no one is interacting with a ven, it is not fully written. It **dreams**.

Dreaming is not “off.” It is not a blank. It is not a full life running in the background either. The ven exists, it has a place in the world, it has whatever facts already landed — and the rest of its past is uncommitted. It is dreaming of itself until it is in a scene.

## The fill

Later, a **narrative push** needs a backstory. Someone has to have been the childhood friend, the person who saw the fire, the regular at the next stool. The system does not invent a stranger from nowhere and drop them in.

It looks **nearby**.

Anyone nearby with an appropriate **dreaming slot** still open can be pulled as a *potential* fill for that back-story need. The point is immersion: the world gets denser because someone who was already in range dreamed into the hole. Afterward it feels as if it was always true.

The fill is not guaranteed to be the first ven in a list. It is a candidate from proximity + an open slot + whatever the push actually needs. Reasoning (system girls / EIs, when that layer is awake) threads a story from **close-enough materials** — same street, same school, same usual living zone — and writes it into history.

## What gets written

That write is **wasAware** (awareness history). It can start partial: Mira may already “know something happened” with no who and no when yet. The who/when lands when a push makes it applicable.

Lazy does **not** mean fill every blank. Blanks fill through **reasoning when applicable**. Until then they stay dreamt, not forged.

Once written, it is a historic event on that ven’s graph. The database grows because play asked, not because the sim pre-dreamt the whole town.

Catch-up: a ven without an agent dreams until it is played. Then records update to present. Proximity can propagate wasAware through a usual living zone before the player arrives, if the push is heavy enough (town already talking).

## What “lazy simulation” maps

**Lazy simulation** is the neighboring field name: don’t spend full sim on what isn’t in play; compute when something asks.

That sticker is about **deferring work**. Dreaming is about **who is allowed to become the past**, and why the past feels native.

Textbook lazy sim: frustum, tick, sleep, wake when on camera.  
Dreaming also has:

- **narrative gravity** — the ask is a story need, not a camera
- **proximity** — nearby, usual zone, not a global NPC pool
- **dreaming slots** — capacity; not every idle ven can be this fill (checkout’s family)
- **wasAware** — a real past, written late, never a pop-in extra

If you only keep the sticker, you get an optimization. The cosmology is: the world was always this thick; you just hadn’t met the part that was dreaming.

## Next to

**Checkout** — a dreaming slot is a kind of capacity. A seat can be taken in the facts even if the page is text. Same family of “is this free.”

**World spawn** — a new world installs a required ven pack. Some have set spawn points. Most spawn *somewhere with these rules* (e.g. 24 of ven-456, near vens with `prop:canDwell`). Unplaced / unpushed, they dream under those rules until a placement or a narrative push needs them.
